Code, Law
and Capital

Khurram Chughtai

Code determines what an arrangement permits, records and prevents. Law supplies the sources through which authority, rights, duties and remedies arise. Capital directs attention to claims upon future value and the distribution of gains and losses. Two editions, published independently and released jointly.

Global Edition

The Global Digital-Assets Order

Examines the relations among code, law and capital in the global order of digital assets. The comparative method follows the asset or claim, the activity, the actor, the infrastructure and the point at which practical control is exercised, putting common questions to different legal orders without assuming that one regulatory model fits them all.

Pakistan Library Edition

The Future of Digital Assets in Pakistan

A national treatise on the legal order of digital and virtual assets in Pakistan: the digital, electronic and ICT legal architecture, the Virtual Assets Act 2026 and PVARA, the monetary, financial, property, integrity and fiscal perimeters, and the institutions, rights and remedies through which those rules take effect.
