F. No. 2(1)/2026-Pub.—The following Ordinance Promulgated on  
2nd June, 2026 by the President is hereby published for general information: - 
 
ORDINANCE NO. IV OF 2026 
 
AN 
 
ORDINANCE 
 
to provide for establishment of Defence Industrial Production  
and Regulatory Authority 
 
WHEREAS it is expedient to establish a Defence Industrial Production and 
Regulatory Authority to supervise, oversee and regulate defence industrial sector 
of Pakistan for making it an important segment of national development, national 
security, defence preparedness, technological sovereignty and national strategic 
interest to resultantly enhance Pakistan’s stature in regional and global defence 
markets;
