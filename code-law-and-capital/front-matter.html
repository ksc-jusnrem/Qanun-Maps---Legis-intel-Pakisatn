Author’s Note

7
This Treatise grew from the KSC.JUSNREM research project The State of Digital Assets in Pakistan. Its present form, Code, Law and Capital, examines digital assets as a problem of legal order: how technical architecture creates and redistributes control, how public and private law allocate authority, rights and remedies, and how value, risk and loss move through institutions and markets.
8
Pakistan is the principal jurisdiction and institutional case. Comparative law, global standards, public international law, private international law, technical architecture and market practice are used for distinct purposes. They illuminate Pakistan’s position; they do not displace Pakistani constitutional and statutory authority or acquire domestic legal force merely by being influential.
9
The law is stated as at 21 August 2026.
10
The Treatise distinguishes enacted and operative law; enacted but uncommenced or conditionally commenced provisions; repealed, spent, lapsed, saved and transitional instruments; delegated legislation and directions made under identified parent powers; consultation drafts, bills, proposals and working texts; judgments and orders according to court, date and precedential setting; official statements, websites and institutional records that evidence administrative action but do not themselves create law; international standards and comparative materials; technical and empirical sources; and the Author’s own analysis and recommendations.
11
The Pakistan Virtual Asset Services Regulations 2026 and the Pakistan Virtual Asset Activity-Specific Regulations 2026 are treated as notified instruments and as the current regulatory baseline. Earlier consultation drafts are used only where they illuminate development history or a change between draft and notified text. Notification is not treated as proof that every licensing, supervisory or market-access function is fully operational.
12
A no-objection certificate, application, provisional permission, sandbox admission, website listing or public announcement is not described as a full licence unless the controlling law establishes that consequence. Likewise, tax disclosure, a bank transfer or possession of a cryptographic key may be evidence for a particular purpose; none is treated, without more, as conclusive proof of title, legality, regulatory permission or immunity.
13
Sources are used according to their legal and evidential function. Primary legal propositions are anchored in constitutions, legislation, notified instruments and binding judgments. Official institutional materials, global standards, scholarship, technical materials, market data and web-native records are identified as such. The citation system follows OSCOLA 5th Edition, adapted for Pakistani legislation, Gazette material, judgments and official repositories. Formal publication identity—such as an Act or S.R.O. number, report series, ISBN, ISSN or DOI—takes priority over a download URL.
14
This Treatise is intended principally for judges, lawyers, regulators and policymakers, while remaining intelligible to technologists, economists, financial institutions, market participants, researchers and students. It is scholarship, not individual legal, investment, tax or financial advice.
