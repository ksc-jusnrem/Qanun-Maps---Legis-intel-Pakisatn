THE DERA GHAZI KHAN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (REPEAL) ACT 
2017 
(XVIII of 2017) 
[08 November 2017] 
An 
Act 
to repeal the Dera Ghazi Khan Development Authority Act, 1991  
It is necessary to repeal the Dera Ghazi Khan Development Authority Act, 
1991 (VI of 1991) so that Dera Ghazi Khan Development Authority may be 
constituted under the provisions of the Development of Cities Act 1976 comparable 
with the Authorities constituted in many other cities of the Punjab inter alia for orderly 
and planned growth of the city of Dera Ghazi Khan. 
Be it enacted by the Provincial Assembly of the Punjab as follows:– 
1. Short title and commencement .– This Act may be cited as the Dera Ghazi 
Khan Development Authority (Repeal) Act 2017. 
(2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Repeal of the Dera Ghazi Khan Development Authority Act, 1991 .– The 
Dera Ghazi Khan Development Authority Act, 1991 (VI of 1991) is hereby repealed. 
3. Savings.– Notwithstanding repeal of the Dera Ghazi Khan Deve lopment 
Authority Act, 1991 (VI of 1991), any rules, regulations or appointments made, 
orders passed, notifications issued, contracts entered into, proceedings commenced, 
rights acquired, liabilities incurred, penalties, rates, fees or charges levied, thin gs 
done  or action taken under penalties, rates, fees or charges levied, things done or 
action taken under any provisions of the repealed Act shall continue to have effect 
unless repealed, recalled, withdrawn or cancelled. 
4. Repeal.– The Dera Ghazi Khan D evelopment Authority (Repeal)  Ordinance 
2017 (X of 2017) is hereby repealed. 
                                                 
1This Act was passed by the Provincial Assembly of the Punjab on 25 October 2017; assented to by the Governor of the Punjab 
on 30 October 2017; and, was published in the Punjab Gazette (Extraordinary), dated: 08 November 2017, pp. 3137 – 3138.
