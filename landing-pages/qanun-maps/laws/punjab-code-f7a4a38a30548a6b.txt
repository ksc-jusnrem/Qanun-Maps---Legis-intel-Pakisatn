THE PROVINCIAL ASSEMBLY OF THE PUNJAB SECRETARIAT 
SERVICES (REPEAL AND REVIVAL) ACT 2022 
(XIV of 2022) 
    [16 August 2022] 
 
An 
Act 
to repeal the Provincial Assembly of the Punjab Secretariat Services Act 2022 and revive the 
Provincial Assembly of the Punjab Secretariat Services Act 2019. 
WHEREAS, in the interest of Parliamentary Supremacy, it is expedient to revive the 
Provincial Assembly of the Punjab Secretariat Services Act 2019 , in the manner hereinafter 
appearing;   
Be it enacted by the Provincial Assembly of the Punjab as follows; 
1. Short title and commencement .− (1) This Act may be called the Provincial 
Assembly of the Punjab Secretariat Services (Repeal and Revival) Act, 2022. 
 (2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Repeal of Act XI of 2022 .− The Provincial Assembly of the Punjab Secretariat 
Services Act 2022 is hereby repealed. 
3. Revival o f Act IX of 2019 .− The Provincial Assembly of the Punjab Secretariat 
Services Act 2019  is hereby revived with effect from 24 th June, as if it had never been 
repealed. 
                                                      
1This Act was passed by Provincial Assembly of the Punjab on 31 July 2022; and was deemed to have been 
assented to by Governor of the Punjab in terms of Article 116(3) of the Constitution; and was published in the 
Punjab Gazette (Extraordinary), dated: 16 August 2022, pp.141.
