THE PUNJAB BUS STANDS AND TRAFFIC CONTROL (GUJRANWALA) 
ORDINANCE, 1963 (XX of 1963) (REPEAL) ACT 2011 
(I of 2012) 
[3rd January, 2012] 
An 
Act 
to repeal the Punjab Bus Stands and Traffic Control (Gujranwala) Ordinance, 1963 
(XX of 1963). 
Preamble.– Whereas it is expe dient to repeal the Punjab Bus Stands and Traffic 
Control (Gujranwala) Ordinance, 1963 (XX of 1963); 
 It is enacted as follows:– 
1. Short title and commencement.– (1) This Act may be called the Punjab Bus 
Stands and Traffic Control (Gujranwala) Ordinance, 1963 (XX of 1963) (Repeal) Act 
2011. 
 (2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Repeal of Ordinance XX of 1963.– The Punjab Bus Stands and Traffic Control 
(Gujranwala) Ordinance, 1963 (XX of 1963) is hereby repealed. 
                                                           
1This Act was passed by the Punjab Assem bly on 21 st December 2011; assented to by the Governor of the Punjab on 31 st 
December 2011; and published in the Punjab Gazette (Extraordinary), dated 3 January 2012, page 39115.
