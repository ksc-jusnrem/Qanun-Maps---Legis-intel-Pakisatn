THE PREVENTION OF CORRUPTION ACT, (WEST PAKISTAN 
EXTENSION) ORDINANCE, 1958.  
 
(West Pakistan Ordinance No. XXXI of 1958) 
 
[20th December, 1958.] 
 
An Ordinance to extend the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947, to the 
whole of the Province of West Pakistan. 
 
 WHEREAS it is expedient to extend the Prevention of Corruption 
Act, 1947, to the whole of the Province of West Pakistan in the manner 
hereinafter appearing; 
 
Preamble. 
 NOW, THEREFORE , in pursuance of the Presidential 
Proclamation of the seventh day of October, 1958, and in exercise of all 
powers enabling him in that behalf, the Governor of West Pakistan is 
pleased to make and promulgate the following Ordinance:— 
 
1. (1) This Ordinance may be called the Prevention of Corruption Act 
(West Pakistan Extension) Ordinance, 1958. 
 
Short title. 
2. The Prevention of Corruption Act, 1947, (hereinafter called the 
said Act), shall extend to the whole of the 1[Province of the Khyber 
Pakhtunkhwa] except the Tribal Areas.
