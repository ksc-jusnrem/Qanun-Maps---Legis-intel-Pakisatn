factory shall be intimated, in writing to the prescribed authority by both the p revious 
and the new occupier, within thirty days of the date on which the change takes place. 
 (2) On a change in the occupancy of any factory– 
(a) the previous occupier shall hand over to the new occupier the registers 
maintained in respect of the factory under section 8 and obtain from the 
new occupier a receipt in this behalf; and

provisions of this sub -section or  in maintaining the registers  in 
accordance with the provisions of section 8.
