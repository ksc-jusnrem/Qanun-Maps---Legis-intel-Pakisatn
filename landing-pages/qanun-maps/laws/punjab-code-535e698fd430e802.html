Preamble.– Whereas it is expedient to provide for the establishment of the 
4
[Superior 
University], Lahore in the private sector and matters connected therewith and ancillary 
thereto; 
It is hereby enacted as follows:– 
1. Short title and commencement .– (1) This Act may be called the Superior 
5
[University], Lahore, Act 2004. 
(2) It shall come into force at once.

context– 
(a) “Board” means the Board of Governors; 
6
[(b) “Chairman” means Chairman Board of Governors;] 
(c) “Company” means SAEPN (Pvt) Limited, registered under the 
Companies Ordinance, 1984 (XLVIII of 1984); 
(d) “Government” means the Government of the Punjab; 
7
[(e) “Patron” means Patron of the University;] 
(g) “Regulations” mean the Regulations made or deemed t o have been 
made under this Act

[3. Establishment of the University.– (1) There shall be established a University 
to be called the Superior University, Lahore , in the privat e sector with its principal 
campus located at Lahore. 
(2) The University shall be a body corporate having perpetual succession 
and a common seal with power to acquire, hold and dispose of property and shall by 
the said name sue and be sued.] 
11
[4. Functions and powers of the University].– The functions and powers of the 
12
[University] shall be– 
(a) to provide for instruction and training in– 
13
[(i) Faculties of Business and Management Sciences, Economics 
and Commerce, Computer Science and Information Technology, 
law, Sciences, Engineering and Technology, Medical Science, 
Allied Health Sciences, Social Sciences, Arts and Humanities, 
Art and Design, Pharmacy, Agriculture and Veterinary Sciences; 
and] 
14
[(ii) such other faculties and their allied branches of knowle dge as 
the Board may approve, on the recommendation of the 
Academic Council and, where applicable, subject to prior 
approval of the relevant statutory body of professional 
education: 
           Provided that Faculties to be established later on, shall be 
approved, on the recommendations of the Accreditation 
Committee constituted under section 12 of the Punjab Higher 
Education Commission Act 2014 (I of 2015);] 
Provided that in case of medical and engineering education, approval of the 
Pakistan Medical & Dental Council and Pakistan Engineering Council respectively shall 
be a pre requisite; 
(b) to provide for research, demonstration and other services, and for the 
advancement and dissemination of knowledge; 
(c) to hold examinations in a manner as may be determi ned by the 
Government and confer on or award degrees, diplomas, certificates

Province of the Punjab.

6.  Patron.– (1) The Governor of the Punjab sh all be the Patron of the 
19
[University]. 
(2) The Patron or his nominee shall preside at  the convoca tions of the 
20
[University]. 
(3) Every proposal to confer an honorary degree shall be subject to 
confirmation by the Patron. 
7. Inspection and inquiry.– (1) The Patron may cause an inspection or inquiry 
to be made in respect of any matter connected with the affairs of the 
21
[University]. 
(2) The Patron shall communicate to the Board his views with regard to the 
result of the inspection or inquiry and shall, after ascertaining the views of the Board, 
advise the Board on the action to be taken by it.

Provided that no action under sub -section (5) shall be taken unless the 
Chairman of the Board is afforded an opportunity of being heard. 
(6) The Government may make Rules to give effect to the provisions of 
sub-section (5). 
(7) The Patron shall have the authority to annul any decision or action of 
the Board, which he thinks was taken against the interest of academic excellence, 
religious and cultural ideology and national integrity. 
8. Board of Governors .– (1) The Board of Governors of the 
24
[University] shall 
consist of the following:– 
(i) the Chairman of the Company; 
(ii) members of the Board of Directors of the Company subject to a 
maximum of four members; 
(iii) Chairman, Higher Education Commission or his nomi nee not below the 
rank of a Director; 
(iv) a Vice Chancellor of a public sector University in the Punjab nominated 
by the Patron; 
(v) the Rector; and 
(vi) Secretary to the Government of the Punjab, Education Department or 
his nominee not below the rank of Additional Secretary. 
(2) The Chairman of the Board of Directors of the Company shall be the 
Chairman of the Board. 
(3) The quorum for a meeting of the Board shall be one half of the total 
number of members, a fraction being counted as one; provided that a t least two 
members shall be official members.

9. Rector.– (1) The Rector shall be appointed by the Government, who shall be an 
eminent scholar of national repute, on such terms and conditions as may be determined 
on the recommendations of the Board. 
(2) The Rector shall perform such functions as are assigned to him by the 
Board. 
10. Powers and functions of the Board of Governors .– The administration and 
management of the affairs of the 
25
[University] shall vest in the Board. 
11. Business of the Board.– (1) The meetings and the business of the Board shall 
be conducted in such manner and in accordance with such procedure as may be 
prescribed in the Regulations and until these matters are prescribed, as may be 
determined by the Board. 
(2) No Regulation or proce eding of the Board shall be invalid by reason of 
any vacancy or defect in the constitution of the Board. 
12. Delegation of powers .– The Board may delegate to any person or a 
committee any of its powers, duties or functions. 
13. Committees.– The Board may c onstitute such committees as may be 
deemed necessary for carrying out the purposes of this Act. 
14. Appointments.– The 
26
[University] may appoint such persons in its services 
as may be necessary on such terms and conditions as may be determined by the 
Board. 
15. 
27
[University] Fund.– There shall be a fund to be known as the Superior 
28
[University],  
Lahore Fund which shall vest in the 
29
[University] and to which shall be credited all sums 
received by the 
30
[University]. 
16. Budget, audit and accounts .– The budge t of the 
31
[University] shall be 
approved and its accounts shall be maintained and audited in such manner as may be 
prescribed by the Board.

17. Regulations.– The Board may, by notification, make Regulations for the 
administration and management of the affairs of the 32[University]. 
18. Removal of difficulties .– If any difficulty arises in giving effect to any of the 
provisions of this Act, the Board may, in consultation with the Government, give such 
directions, not inconsistent with the said provisions, as it may consider necessary for 
the removal of such difficulty.
