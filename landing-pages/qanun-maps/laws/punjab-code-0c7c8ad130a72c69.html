THE PUNJAB FINANCE ACT 1998 
(VII of 1998) 
[30th June, 1998] 
An 
Act 
to levy and enhance the rates of certain taxes, duties and fees in the Punjab. 
Preamble.– Whereas, it is expedient to levy and enhance the rates of c ertain taxes, 
duties and fees in the Punjab; 
 It is hereby enacted as follows:– 
1. Short title, extent and commencement .– (1) This Act may be called the 
Punjab Finance Act 1998. 
 (2) It shall extend to whole of the Punjab. 
 (3) It shall come into force on and from the first day of July 1998.
