THE PAKISTAN MILITARY ACADEMY (DEGREES AND CERTIFICATES) 
ORDINANCE, 1959 
ORDINANCE No. LI of 1959 
[16th September, 1959]  
An Ordinance to authorise the Pakistan Military Academy at Kakul to confer degrees and grant 
certificates. 
 WHEREAS it is  expedient to authorise the Pakistan Military Academy at Kakul to confer 
degrees and grant certificates;  
 NOW, THEREFORE, in pursuance of the Proclamation of the seventh day of October, 1958, 
and in exercise of all powers enabling him in that behalf, the President is pleased to make and 
promulgate the following Ordinance:___  
 1. Short title and commencement.___(1) This Ordinance may be called the Pakistan Military 
Academy (Degrees and Certificates) Ordinance, 1959.  
 (2) It shall come into force at once.  
 2. Definition. In this Ordinance, unless there is anything repugnant in the subject or 
context,___  
(a)  “Academy” means the Pakistan Military Academy at Kakul;  
(b)  “certificate” means a certificate mentioned in Part II of the Schedule; and  
(c)  “degree” means a degree mentioned in Part I of the Schedule.  
 3. Conferment of degrees, etc., on cadets and officers. ___1[(1)] The Academy shall have the 
power to confer a degree on, or grant a certificate to, any gentleman cadet of the Academy on his 
successful completion of the appropriate course at the Academy, and such power shall include the 
power to confer a degree on, or grant a certificate to, any officer who at any time before the 
commencement of this Ordinance was commissioned from the Academy.  
 1[(2) The provisions of sub -section (1) shall apply to those gentlemen cadets of the Academy 
who, before the commencement of the Pakistan Military Academy (Degrees and Certificates) 
(Amendment) Ordinance, 1962, had successfully completed their training at the Royal Military 
Academy, Sandhurst, or who , after such commencement, successfully complete their training at that 
Academy.]  
 4. Power to make regulations. ___The Academy may, with the previous approval in writing 
of the 2[Federal Government], make Regulations for carrying out the purposes of this Ordinance.  
 
1Re-numbered and added by ordinance No. XIX of 1962, s.2. 
2Subs. by F.A.O., 1975.
