THE PROVINCIAL ASSEMBLY OF THE PUNJAB PRIVILEGES (REPEAL 
AND REVIVAL) ACT 2022 
(XVI of 2022) 
    [30 August 2022] 
 
An 
Act 
to repeal the Provincial Assembly of the Punjab Privileges Act 2022 (XII of 2022) and 
revive the Provincial Assembly of the Punjab Privileges Act 1972 (II of 1972). 
WHEREAS, in the interest of Parliamentary Supremacy, it is expedient to revive the 
Provincial Assembly of the Punjab Privileges Act 1972 (II of 1972), in the manner 
hereinafter appearing;   
Be it enacted by the Provincial Assembly of the Punjab as follows; 
1. Short title and commencement. − (1) This Act may be called the Provincial 
Assembly of the Punjab Privileges (Repeal and Revival) Act, 2022. 
 (2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Repeal of Act XII of 2022.− The Provincial Assembly of the Punjab Privileges 
Act 2022 is hereby repealed. 
3. Revival of Act II of 1972. -  The Provincial Assembly of the Punjab Privileges 
Act 1972 is hereby revived with effect from 24 th June, 2022, as if it had never been 
repealed. 
                                                      
1This Act was passed by Provincial Assembly of the Punjab on 16 August 2022; and was deemed to have been assented to by 
Governor of the Punjab in terms of Article 116(3) of the Constitution; and was published in the Punjab Gazette (Extraordinary), 
dated: 30 August 2022, pp.249.
