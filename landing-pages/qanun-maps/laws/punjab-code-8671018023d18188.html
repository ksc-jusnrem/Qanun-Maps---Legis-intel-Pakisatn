1THE PUNJAB SHOPS AND ESTABLISHMENTS (SECURITY) ACT 1999
(II of 1999)
[4th February, 1999]
1This Act was passed by the Punjab Assembly on 28th January, 1999; assented to by the Governor of the Punjab on 1st  
February, 1999; and, was published in the Punjab Gazette (Extraordinary), dated 4th February, 1999, page 263, which is  
repealed by the Punjab Security of Vulnerable Establishments Ordinance 2015 (I of 2015), w.e.f. 8.1.2015, s.20; and published  
in the Punjab Gazette (Extraordinary), pages 2077-2081.
