THE PAKISTAN INTERNATIONAL AIRLINE CORPORATION 
(SUSPENSION OF TRADE UNIONS AND EXISTING AGREEMENTS) 
ORDER (REPEAL) ACT, 2008
ACT No. II OF 2008 
[28th August, 2008] 
An Act to repeal the Pakistan International Airline Corporation (Suspension of Trade Unions and 
Existing Agreements) Order 2001 
 WHEREAS it is expedient to repeal the Pakistan International Airline Corporation (Suspension 
of Trade Unions and Existing Agreements) Order, 2001 (C.E's Order No. 6 of 2001);  
 It is hereby enacted as follows:— 
 1. Short title and commencement.___ (1) This Act may be called the Pakistan International 
Airline  Corporation (Suspension of Trade Unions and Existing Agreements) Order (Repeal) Act, 
2008.  
 (2) It shall come into force at once.  
 2. Repeal of the Pakistan International Airline Corporation (Suspension of Trade 
Unions and Existing Agreements) Order, 2001 (C.E's Order No. 6 of 2001).___(1) The Pakistan 
International Airline Corporation (Suspension of Trade Unions and Existing Agreements) Order 
2001, (C.E's Order No. 6 of 2001) is hereby repealed: 
 Provided that the trade unions, associations, organizations or groups of any type of employees 
suspended under the aforesaid Order shall be registered afresh in accordance with law. 
 (2) Notwithstanding the repeal of the C. E's Order No. 6 of 2001, and without prejudice to 
the provisions of section 6 and 24 of the General Clauses Act, 1897 (X of 1897), all administrative 
orders issued, actions taken and modifications made in settlements shall continue in force until altered 
or rescinded.
