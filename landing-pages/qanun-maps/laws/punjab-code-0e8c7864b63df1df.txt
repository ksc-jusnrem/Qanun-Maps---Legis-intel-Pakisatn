THE PUBLIC GAMBLING (REPEAL) ACT, 1985  
(II of 1985) 
[7th November, 1985] 
An 
Act 
to repeal the Public Gambling Act, 1867 (Act III of 1867). 
Preamble.– WHEREAS it is expedient to repeal the Public Gambling Act, 1867 (III of 
1867); 
 It is hereby enacted as follows:– 
1. Short title and commencement .– (1) This Act shall be called t he Public 
Gambling (Repeal) Act, 1985. 
 (2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Repeal of Act III of 1867 .– The Public Gambling Act, 1867 (III of 1867) is 
hereby repealed.
