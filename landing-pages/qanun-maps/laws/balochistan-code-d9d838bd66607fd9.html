to provide for the levy of a tax on services provided, rendered, initiated,

CHAPTER–I 
PRELIMINARY 
 
1.  Short Title, Extent and Commencement. -- (1) This Act shall be called the Balochistan 
Sales Tax on Services Act 1[,] 2015. 
 
(2) It extends to the whole of Balochistan 2[***]. 
 
(3) This Act shall come into force on and from 1st day of July, 2015. 
 
(4) Notwithstanding sub -section (3), the Government may, by notification in the 
official Gazette, specify services or class or classes of services in respect of which tax shall be 
collected and paid with effect from the date subsequent to and different from the date of the 
commencement of this Act.
