1THE PUNJAB PRIVATIZATION BOARD (REPEAL) ACT 2021 
  (XXX of 2021) 
    [01 October 2021] 
 
An  
Act  
to repeal the Punjab Privatization Board Act 2010 
It is necessary to repeal the Punjab Privatization Board Act 2010 (IV of 2010) for the 
purposes hereinafter appearing. 
Be it enacted by Provincial Assembly of the Punjab as follows: 
1. Short title and commencement .– (1) This Act may be cited as the Punjab 
Privatization Board (Repeal) Act 2021. 
 (2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Repeal and saving .– (1) The Punjab Privatization Board  Act 2010 (IV of 
2010) is hereby repealed. 
 (2) Notwithstanding the repeal of the Punjab Privatization Board Act 2010 
(IV of 2010) , all the proceedings and processes pertaining to privatization of the 
Government properties, initiated or completed, assessment of prices of properties 
made, auctions done, sanction orders issued, any appointment made, order passed, 
notification issued, contracts executed, rights acquired, liabilities incurred, possession 
of properties delivered and proceedings initiated  before various courts or other 
forums and things done or action taken under any provision of the repealed Act shall 
continue to have effect unless repealed, recalled, withdrawn or cancelled. 
3. Transition.– (1) All the properties, both urban and rural, i ncluding agricultural 
land, whether occupied and unoccupied, placed at the disposal of defunct Punjab 
Privatization Board, which may be available for disposal immediately before the 
repeal of the aforesaid Act, shall stand transferred to the Colonies Department, Board 
of Revenue, Punjab. 
(2) The Secretary and other employees of the defunct Punjab Privatization 
Board, whether permanent or contractual, shall stand transferred to the Colonies 
Department, Board of Revenue, Punjab. 
(3) All the moveable assets of  defunct Punjab Privatization Board shall 
stand transferred to Colonies Department, Board of Revenue, Punjab.
