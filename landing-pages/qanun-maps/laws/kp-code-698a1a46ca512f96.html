THE 1[KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA]

(2[KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA] ACT NO. III OF 1989) 
 
[First published after having received the assent of the Governor of the 3[Khyber Pakhtunkhwa] 
in the Gazette of 4[Khyber Pakhtunkhwa] (Extraordinary), dated the 28th May, 1989]. 
 
AN 
ACT 
to repeal Martial Law Order No. 362, issued by the 
Marital Law Administrator, Zone ‘B’. 
 
 Preamble.---WHEREAS it is expedient to repeal Martial Law Order No. 362, issued by 
the Martial Law Administrator, Zone ‘B’. 
 
 It is hereby enacted as follows: 
 
1. Short title and commencement. ---(1) This Act may be called the 5[Khyber 
Pakhtunkhwa] (Repeal of Martial Law Order) Act, 1989. 
 
 (2) It shall come into force at once. 
 
2. Repeal of M.L.O. 362. ---Martial Law Order No. 362, issued by the Martial Law 
Administrator, Zone ‘B’, is hereby repealed.
