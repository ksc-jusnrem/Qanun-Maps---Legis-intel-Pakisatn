THE RACE COURSES LICENSING (REPEAL) ORDINANCE, 1985  
(XXIII of 1985) 
[23rd February, 1985] 
An 
Ordinance 
to repeal the Race Courses Licensing Act, 1912 
Preamble.– Whereas it is expedient to repeal the Race Courses Licensing Act, 1912 (III 
of 1912); 
 Now, therefore, in pursuance of the Proclamation of the fifth d ay of July, 1977, 
read with the Laws (Continuance in Force) Order, 1977 (C.M.L.A. Order No. 1 of 1977), 
and the Provisional Constitution Order, 1981 (C.M.L.A. Order No. 1 of 1981), the 
Governor of the Punjab is pleased to make and promulgate the following Ordinance:- 
1. Short title and commencement .– (1) This Ordinance may be called the Race 
Courses Licensing (Repeal) Ordinance, 1985. 
 (2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Repeal of Act No. III of 1912 .– The Race Courses Licensing Act, 1912 (Act No. 
III of 1912), is hereby repealed.
