(Extraordinary), dated the 24th July, 2005]. 
 
AN 
ACT 
to repeal the North-West Frontier Province  Agricultural Research 
System (Handing Over) Act, 1986 (NWFP Act No. VIII of 1987). 
 
WHEREAS it is expedient to repeal the North-West Frontier Province  Agricultural 
Research System (Handing Over) Act, 1986 (NWFP Act No. VIII of 1987) for the purposes 
hereinafter appearing; 
 
It is hereby enacted as follows:- 
 
1. Short title and commencement:-(1) This Act may be called the 5[Khyber 
Pakhtunkhwa] Agricultural Research System (Handing Over) (Repeal) Act, 2005. 
 
(2)       It shall come into force at once. 
 
2. Repeal of NWFP Act No. VIII of 1987. -The NWFP Agricultural Research System  
(Handing Over) Act, 1986 (NWFP Act No. VIII of 1987) is hereby repealed. 
 
3. Savings.-(1) Notwithstanding the repeal of the North West Frontier Province 
Agricultural Research System (Handing Over) Act, 1986 (NWFP Act No. VIII of 1987), any 
scheme prepared or executed, Research made contract entered into, suit instituted, rights 
acquired, claims made, legal or administrative proceedings or action taken under the said 
Act shall be deemed to have been validly prepared or executed, entered into, instituted, 
acquired, made or taken, 
 
(2)       The assets and liabilities, if any, acquired under the repealed Act shall 
henceforth be the assets and liabilities of the Agriculture Department.
