THE WEST PAKISTAN VALIDATION OF NAIB TEHSILDARS AND NAIB 
TEHSILDAR CANDIDATES SELECTION ORDINANCE, 1962 
(VI of 1962) 
[11 April 1962] 
An 
Ordinance 
to validate the selection of Naib Tehsildars and Naib Tehsildar candidates. 
Preamble.– WHEREAS it is expedient to validate the selection of Naib Tehsildars 
and Naib Tehsildar candidates made during the years 1960 and 1961; 
 NOW, THEREFORE,  in pursuance of  the Presidential Proclamation of the 
seventh day of October, 1958, and in exercise of all powers enabling him in that 
behalf, the Governor of West Pakistan is pleased to make and promulgate the 
following Ordinance:– 
1. Short title .– This Ordinance may be called the West Pakistan Validation of 
Naib Tehsildars and Naib Tehsildar Candidates Selection Ordinance, 1962. 
[2.]
2
 Definitions.– In this Ordinance, unless the context otherwise requires, the 
following expressions shall have the meanings hereby respectiv ely assigned to 
them, that is to say– 
(a) “existing rules” means– 
(i) The Punjab Tehsildari Service Rules, 1953; 
(ii) The Bahawalpur State Tehsildari Service Rules, 1941; 
(iii) The rules for the selection of Naib Tehsildar candidates in the 
Quetta Division  published with the late Agent to the Governor -
General and Chief Commissioner in Baluchistan’s Order No. E-
101/51, dated the 25th/26th December 1951; and 
(iv) The Kalat State Services Punishment and Appeal Rules, 1945; 
(b) “Government” means the Government of West Pakistan. 
3. Validation of selection of Naib Tehsildars and Naib Tehsildar 
candidates.– (1) Notwithstanding anything contained in the existing rules governing 
the selection or appointment of Naib Tehsildars and Naib Tehsildar candidates, or in 
any decree, order or judgment of a Court, the persons specified in column 3 of Part 
A of the Schedule shall be deemed to have been validly selected as Naib Tehsildars, 
and the persons specified in 
3
