THE HOUSE BUILDING FINANCE CORPORATION (REPEAL) ACT, 2018 
ACT NO. XXV OF 2018 
[18th May, 2018] 
An Act to repeal the House Building Finance Corporation Act, 1952 
            WHEREAS it is expedient to repeal the House Building Finance Corporation Act, 1952 (XVIII 
of 1952), for the purposes hereinafter appealing; 
It is hereby enacted as follows:— 
            1. Short title and commencement. ___ (1) This Act may be called the House Building 
Finance Corporation (Repeal) Act, 2018. 
(2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Repeal.- The House Building Finance Corporation (Repeal) Act, 1952 (XVIII of 1952) 
is hereby repealed. 
 
 
______________ 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
RGN. Dated. 01/06/2026
