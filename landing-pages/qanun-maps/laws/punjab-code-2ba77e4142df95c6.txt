THE PUNJAB FINANCE ACT 2024 
(VIII of 2024) 
[27th June, 2024] 
An 
Act 
to levy, alter and rationalize certain taxes, fees and duties in the Punjab. 
It is necessary in public interest to levy, alter and rationalize certain taxes, fees and 
duties in the Punjab; and, to deal with ancillary matters. 
Be it enacted by Provincial Assembly of the Punjab as follows: 
1. Short title, extent and commencement.– (1) This Act may be cited as the 
Punjab Finance Act 2024. 
 (2) This Act extends to the whole of the Punjab. 
 (3) This Act shall come into force on the first day of July 2024 except the 
provisions of section 4 which shall come into force on such date as the Government 
may, by notification in the official Gazette, specify.
