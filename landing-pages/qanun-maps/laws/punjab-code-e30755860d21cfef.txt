4. Seizure of tobacco smoked by a juvenile in a public place.– If any juvenile 
be found smoking tobacco in any public place, it shall be lawful for any teacher or 
proctor of a recognized school or affiliated college or any other person authorised
2
 by 
Government in this behalf, to seize such tobacco and destroy it.
