THE MARTIAL LAW ORDER No.1371 (REPEAL) ACT, 1989  
(VI of 1989) 
[9th April, 1989] 
An 
Act 
to repeal the Martial Law  Order No.1371, dated February 9, 1984 issued by MLA Zone 
‘A’ (Punjab) (Ban on Student Unions) 
Preamble.– Whereas it is expedient to repeal th e Martial Law Order No.1371, dated 
February 9, 1984 issued by MLA Zone ‘A’ in the manner hereinafter appearing; 
 It is hereby enacted as follows:– 
1. Short title and commencement .– (1) This Act may be called the Martial Law 
Order No.1371 (Repeal) Act, 1989. 
 (2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Repeal of MLO No.1371.– The Martial Law Order No.1371 dated February 9, 
1984 issued by MLA Zone ‘A’ (Punjab) is hereby repealed. 
3. Repeal of Punjab Ordinance No.II of 1989 .– The Martial Law Order No.1371 
(Repeal) Ordinance, 1989 (Punjab Ordinance No.II of 1989) is hereby repealed. 
                                                                 
1This Act w as passed by the Punjab Assembly on 29th March, 1989; assented to by the Governor of the Punjab on 6th April, 1989; 
and, w as published in the Punjab Gazette (Extraordinary), dated 9th April, 1989, pages 949-A to 949-B.
