THE SHORT TITLES ACT, 1973 
 
Act No. XXI of 1973 
 
 
An Act to assign short titles to certain Martial Law Regulations and Martial Law Orders 
 
WHEREAS clause (3) of Article 280 of the Interim Constitution of the Islamic Republic of 
Pakistan, hereinafter referred to as the Interim Constitution, provides that each of the M artial Law 
Regulations and Martial Law Orders specified in the Seventh Schedule thereto shall be deemed to 
have become an Act of the appropriate Legislature; 
 
AND WHEREAS it is expedient, for facility of  reference, to assign short titles to the 
aforesaid Martial Law Regulations and Martial Law Orders and also some of the Martial Law 
Regulations mentioned in the First Schedule to the Interim Constitution; 
 
And whereas the Proclamation of Emergency referred to in clause (8) of Article 139 of the 
Interim Constitution is in force; 
 
It is hereby enacted as follows:— 
 
1. Short title and commencement.—(1) This Act may be called the Short Titles Act,                                                                                                                                     
1973. 
 
(2) It shall come into force at once. 
 
2. The laws specified in the first two  columns  of  the  Schedule  to  this  Act,  being  laws  
specified  in  the  First  and  the Seventh Schedules to the Interim Constitution, shall be cited and 
referred to by the short titles mentioned against each in the third column of the Schedule to this Act.
