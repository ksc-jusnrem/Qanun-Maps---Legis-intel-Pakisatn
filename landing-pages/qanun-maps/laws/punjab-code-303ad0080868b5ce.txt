THE NAZARIA-I-PAKISTAN FOUNDATION (REPEAL) ACT 2008 
(III of 2008) 
[28th August, 2008] 
An 
Act 
to repeal the Nazaria-i-Pakistan Foundation Act, 1997 (XII of 1997). 
 Whereas it is expedient to repeal the Nazaria -i-Pakistan Foundation Act, 
1997 (XII of 1997) for the purposes hereinafter appearing; 
 It is hereby enacted as follows:– 
1. Short title and commencement.–  (1) This Act may be cited as the Nazaria -
i-Pakistan Foundation (Repeal) Act 2008. 
 (2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Repeal, revival and succession .–  (1) The Nazaria -i-Pakistan Foundation 
Act, 1997 (XII of 1997) is hereby repealed. 
 (2) Notwithstanding the repeal of the Act XII of 1997– 
“(a) the Nazaria -i-Pakistan Trust, registered under the Societies 
Registration Act 1860 (XXI of 1860) which stood dissolved under 
section 13 of the Act, shall stand revived; 
(b) the Nazaria-i-Pakistan Trust shall be governed under the provisions of the 
Societies Registration Act, 1860 (XXI of 1860); 
(c) all rights, properties, assets or liabilities of the Nazaria -i-Pakistan 
Foundation shall stand transferred to the Nazaria-i-Pakistan Trust as its 
rights, properties, assets or liabilities; and 
(d) an action taken, thing done, right acquired, liability imposed or any 
proceedings initiated under the Act shall not be affected.” 
3. Repeal.–  The Nazaria-i-Pakistan Foundation (Repeal) Ordinance 2008 (VI of 
2008) is hereby repealed. 
                                                           
1This Act was passed by the Punjab Assembly on 7 August 2008; assented to by the Governor of the Punjab on 26 August 
2008; and, was published in the Punjab Gazette (Extraordinary), dated 28 August 2008, pages 313-314.
