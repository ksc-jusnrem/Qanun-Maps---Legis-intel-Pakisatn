THE KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA LIMITATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2016. 
(KHYBER PAKHTUNKHWA ACT NO. XV OF 2016) 
 (First published after having received the assent of the Governor of Khyber  
 Pakhtunkhwa  in the Gazette of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ,(Extraordinary), 
dated the 19/08/2016). 
 
AN 
ACT 
further to amend  
the Limitation act, 1908. 
 
WHEREAS it is expedient further to amend the Limitation Act, 1908, for the purpose 
hereinafter appearing;  
 
It is hereby enacted as follows: 
 
1. Short title and commencement .---(1) This Act may be called the Khyber 
Pakhtunkhwa  Limitation (Amendment) Act, 2016. 
 
 (2) It shall come into force at once. 
 
2. Amendment of the First Schedule of the Act No. IX of 1908 .---In the Limitation 
Act, 1908 (Act No. IX of 1908), in the First Schedule, in Part-IX, Articles 147 and 148 shall 
be deleted.
