2. Definition.– In this Ordinance, unless there is anything repugnant in the 
subject or context, the expression “Shamilat” means land described as such in the 
Record of Rights, and the land so described, shall be deemed to be Shamilat, 
notwithstanding that the whole or a part of it is in the possession of one or more of 
the proprietors in the estate, or of any other person.
