THE PUNJAB RELIEF OF INDEBTEDNESS ACT, 1934 
(VII of 1934) 
[12 April 1935] 
An 
Act 
to provide for the Relief of Indebtedness in the Punjab. 
 WHEREAS it is expedient to amend the law governing the relations between debtors 
and creditors; 
 And Whereas the previous sanction of the Governor-General required under section 
80-A(3) of the Government of India Act and the previous sanction of the Governor required 
under section 80-C of the said Act have been obtained; 
 It is hereby enacted as follows:–
