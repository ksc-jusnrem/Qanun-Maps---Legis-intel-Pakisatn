Preamble.– WHEREAS it is expedient to make provision for the establishment of 
Family Courts for the expeditious settlement and disposal of disputes relating to 
marriage and family affairs and for matters connected therewith; 
  It is hereby enacted as follows:– 
1. Short title, extent and commencement.– (1) This Act may be called the 
3

Family Courts Act, 1964. 
 
4

 (3) It shall come into force in such area or areas and on such date or dates 
as Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, specify in this behalf. 
 
5
[(4) Nothing in this Act shall apply to any suit, or any application under the 
Guardians and Wards Act, 1890, p ending for trial or hearing in any Court 
immediately before the coming into force of this Act, and all such suits and 
applications shall be heard and disposed of as if this Act were not in force. 
 (5) Any suit, or any application under the Guardians and Wa rds Act, 1890, 
which was pending for trial or hearing in any Court immediately before the coming 
into force of this Act, and which has been dismissed solely on the ground that such 
suit or application is to be tried by a Family Court established under this  Act, shall, 
notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in any law, on petition made to it 
in that behalf by any party to the suit or application, be tried and heard by such Court 
from the stage at which such suit or application had reached at the time of its 
dismissal.]
